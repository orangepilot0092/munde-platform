# Master Vision

Project Sahyadri is an proprietary data and intelligence platform designed to build digital public infrastructure for Maharashtra. It transforms fragmented government and public data into trusted, reusable, and production-ready intelligence services that power decision-making across multiple domains.

## Core Principles
- **Data First:** Reliable intelligence begins with reliable data.
- **Official Sources First:** Prioritize official government APIs and open data portals.
- **Secure by Design:** Transparent architecture and reproducible pipelines.
- **Reusable Components:** Shared infrastructure over duplicated implementations.
- **Explainable Intelligence:** Evidence-backed recommendations.
- **Production Quality:** Deployable with testing, observability, and security.
